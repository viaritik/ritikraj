Section I · Profile & Foundation

About Me

I'm interested in where commercial law meets new technology, for instance what happens to an arbitration clause when a bill of lading changes hands by cryptographic endorsement instead of by hand.

I am in my second year of the integrated B.A. (Policy Science) LL.B. (Hons.) at National Law University Meghalaya. My courtroom experience so far is in criminal and constitutional work. In a Bhagalpur defence chamber I drafted bail applications, and at the Patna High Court I wrote bench memos on writ petitions and Atrocities Act appeals.

My research centres on international commercial arbitration, maritime law and digital trade. My current manuscript, under peer review with the Journal of International Maritime Law, compares how Indian and English law treat arbitral consent for third-party holders of electronic bills of lading.
